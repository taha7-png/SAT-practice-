Study Notes

Reading & Writing — Craft & Structure

  • Words in Context: use surrounding clues to pick the most precise word.
  • Text Structure: identify how sentences are organised (compare, contrast, cause-effect).
  • Cross-Text Connections: compare two short passages on the same topic.

Standard English Conventions

  • Boundaries: avoid comma splices and run-ons; use semicolons and periods correctly.
  • Form & Sense: subject-verb agreement, pronoun clarity, verb tense consistency.

Math — Formulas with Worked Examples

  • Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
    • Example: Slope through (2,3) and (6,11): m = (11−3)/(6−2) = 8/4 = 2.
  • Quadratic: x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a
    • Example: x² − 5x + 6 = 0 → x = [5 ± √(25−24)]/2 = (5±1)/2 → x = 3 or 2.
  • Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²
    • Example: Centre (2,−3), r=4 → (x−2)² + (y+3)² = 16.
  • Percent change: (new − old) / old × 100
    • Example: $80 → $100 → (100−80)/80 × 100 = 25%.

Expression of Ideas

  • Transitions: However (contrast), Therefore (result), Similarly (agreement).
  • Rhetorical Synthesis: use bullet notes to achieve a specific writing goal.
